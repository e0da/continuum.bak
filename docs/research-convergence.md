# Research integration and historical recovery

The completed local research lineage integrates startup, shared runtime state,
rigid mechanics, scalar/thermal ownership, independent scheduling, rendering
experiments and portable/native consumers. Main is the source checkpoint;
installed builds and private measurements remain separately qualified. The
[current capacity result](compiled-thermal-mass.md) reports exact continuation
and observed costs without promising whole-game performance or fidelity.

Historical branches are classified by ancestry, patch equivalence and actual
successors before cleanup. Obsolete Gimbal-primary prose is superseded by the
current shared direction and provisional repository boundary. Rejected live
owners are not re-enabled merely to preserve their old branch. Every old ref,
dirty draft and nonreconstructable ignored artifact has a private recovery home.

- [Menu shader execution proof](../tools/metal-shader-donor/Assets/MenuShaderExecutionProof.md),

  recovery and compilation do not newly qualify menu or solar-panel rendering.

  Run with `--continuum-input-cadence-census`; optional
  `--continuum-input-cadence-quit` exits after its bounded capture. The scale
  profiler relinquishes its competing autoquit path for that combination.
  Exception-safe Harmony finalization closes the exact callback scope without
  suppressing the original exception. Log counters are diagnostic evidence;
  source recovery does not establish sampling overhead or native qualification.
- [Sleeping-island negative result](sleeping-island-experiment.md), from
  `26150bf`: the report is preserved while its rejected runtime remains retired.

Python chronicle/tooling paths have existing C#/Rust successors. Old KspRigid
paths have current KspContinuum owners. Tangent reuse and the old live dynamics
canary have explicit replacement/removal decisions. Keeping obsolete versions
active would regress current ownership and qualification.

Unfinished historical physics-harness and integrator-chain work remains a draft,
with its original source and base pinned privately. The new draw-isolation draft
is also preserved for the temporal rendering bench. Its next proof must separate
same-shader submission comparisons from original/reconstructed material fidelity;
current faithful mapped-family coverage does not include the solar shaders.
Single images are insufficient to qualify flicker. Main integration does not
resolve the outstanding dark-craft, solar-material, contact or gameplay gates.

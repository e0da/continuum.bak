# Owned diagnostic docking situation

`station-docking` is a selectable reference-program micro-situation. It performs a controlled approach, actual stock docking and one stock undocking operation. `station-undocking` remains a planned independent recipe starting from a merged checkpoint; the roundtrip already observes separation.

Create the private paired seed with [DockingFixture](../tools/KspContinuum.DockingFixture/README.md). Pin the checkpoint, base launch profile and parent receipt hashes in the program inputs. Add `docking` with canonical `activeVessel`, `targetVessel`, nonzero distinct `activePort`/`targetPort` flight IDs, and an explicit `preparePair` boolean. Use `program-prepare --recipe station-docking --inputs INPUTS --output NEW_PLAN` to freeze the current installation, then execute its exact `runArguments`. The addon and runner must both contain this implementation. Retained profiles reject changed installed binaries; after restoring an installation, reinstall the selected laboratory variant and freeze a fresh profile before another run.

`preparePair=true` declares two preparations before measured admission: rotate both source orbits toward the source-epoch Sun, preserving radius, speed and radial velocity; then align actual loaded model port transforms, establish a 2 m gap, equalize velocity and add 0.05 m/s axial closing velocity. Temporary collision suppression is restored synchronously. No repositioning occurs after admission.

The driver owns a scoped replacement of `Vessel.FeedInputFeed` for current vessels containing the retained original Part objects. It sends quiet controls through stock `propagateControlUpdate`, preserving the original physical guards. It leaves all provider registrations intact through vessel merge, destruction and split. Stock physics, capture, joint creation and `ModuleDockingNode.Undock` remain authoritative. Distributed approach acceleration is bounded at 0.01 m/s² and stops during stock acquisition. This is a declared diagnostic controller, not RCS or MechJeb autopilot qualification.

Admission requires loaded, unpacked, initialized compatible Ready ports in Kerbin orbit, normal time, disabled autopilot/SAS/RCS/reaction wheels and no ignited engines. Exact stock data transmitters must have no queued data or active transmission throughout the sequence. Non-EC resources are conserved; EC consumption has a frozen stock-command/docking-motor upper bound. The initial fixture has no crew. Subclassed or unmodeled consumers are rejected.

The camera owns one common stock flight rig before camera/effect updates. It looks from the actual solar direction toward the port-pair center, including after ownership changes. Render callbacks independently verify the admitted pose. Owned method patches, render callbacks, camera poses, scheduling and input lock are restored at termination. A rejected second owner cannot remove the first owner's patches.

`continuum-docking-roundtrip/v1` records explicit identities, original and split part groups, five events (`seed`, `admitted`, `merged`, `before-undock`, `split`), reciprocal port links, a real connected joint, actual separation, resource observations, provider censuses and cleanup. Destroyed old vessels are terminal census entries without a callback-equality claim. The Rust runner validates the receipt against its own run/PID/checkpoint/pair and retains seed, docked and split PNGs. Inspect those pixels separately. Preserve invalid, partial and timed-out attempts.

The 30-second generic flight duration is a loader carrier; the event driver has separate bounded phase and wall deadlines. Generic immutable-topology profiling is disabled for this workload. No FPS, observer-overhead, crewed mission, fuel controller, deterministic replay or save/reload claim follows from a successful roundtrip. Stock/mod shutdown exceptions remain separate findings even when the scoped diagnostic and process exit pass.

# Captured craft rigid laboratory

`NativeAssemblySceneCapture` creates a private immutable physics packet on the Unity thread. The owned Probe observer invokes it after the actual `FixedUpdate.PhysicsFixedUpdate` boundary. Select `diagnostics.nativeAssemblyScene` in a frozen launch profile, or `--flight-native-assembly-scene <new absolute directory>` when resolving a diagnostic. The launcher requires a pinned normal-time flight and passes the path explicitly; ordinary play does not capture it.

Native bodies, authored parts and collision shapes have different identities. The packet deduplicates bodies discovered through part descendants and own/effective/servo aliases. Each part retains its alias ledger; each collider records its actual attached body. Physicsless aliases never add mass. Native binary32 poses/COM/tensors are distinguished from double-composed hierarchy transforms. A synchronous before/after witness rejects changed bodies, aliases, shapes, hierarchy or frame/time identity; binary geometry and manifest publish together to a fresh private directory.

The existing rigid workbench accepts `CONTINUUM_RIGID_WORKBENCH_IMPORT=<private manifest.json>`, together with a fresh `CONTINUUM_RIGID_WORKBENCH_OUTPUT`. It imports active unconstrained dynamic bodies, assigns dense member indices in sorted source-body order, and retains every admitted shape belonging to those bodies, including disabled/inactive shapes. Other bodies and unsupported geometry remain explicit coverage limits. Meshes are shared by geometry index. Collision meshes drive a diagnostic view; this is not the original craft material/rendering stack.

The compound owner sums mass, COM and the complete rotated inertia tensor once per native body. All-body COM velocities and angular velocities supply linear and angular momentum. A full tensor solve determines rigid angular velocity. The lab subtracts the common aggregate velocity as a declared Galilean frame change; it preserves relative motion's contribution to angular momentum. Initialization reports source/projected kinetic energy and the internal motion removed by rigid projection. Captured native world COM and position-plus-local-COM reconstruction are separate observations with a reported residual.

One member may retain zero, one or many colliders without duplicating its mass. The owner can perform an off-center impulse, a closed mass transfer with conserved momentum, and a caller-resolved partition into two bodies. The native receipt includes source body/shape bindings, actual mass/COM/tensors and shape parameters, flags and transforms. The independent verifier uses matrix arithmetic from raw inputs rather than production aggregation/projection code. The real-craft resolved partition is a laboratory transaction, not a staging/docking graph policy.

Contact simulation is disabled for the first imported motion proof. Source convex mesh binding and cooking configuration do not prove equality of recooked hulls, native contact response or friction. Primitive collision components retain original dimensions/scaling inputs; stretched primitive debug meshes are presentation approximations. The packet lacks pending native forces, contact manifolds and solver history, so it is not an exact trajectory restart. Timed capture is intrusive and supplies no clean FPS improvement claim.

Loopback JSON is a low-rate debugging and evidence interface. Simulation steps operate on resident native bodies; they do not serialize motion through that interface each tick. Source float columns must decode as binary32 before promotion to double: the JSON decimal `4.55` represents the original float value, whose exact double expansion is different.

## Fidelity and feel

KSP is a gameplay reference. Physical invariants and independent numerical oracles decide simulation quality; exact stock results are not required. Improved fidelity can change trajectories, damping, authority or response and still improve play. Explain the cause of each divergence before judging it.

Evaluate control/feedback separately: attitude changes, docking alignment, landing and EVA movement, recovery from mistakes, predictability and enjoyment. The acceptable threshold for the game's feel is discovered by playing these situations; do not invent a numerical stock-equivalence budget for it. Preserve useful familiar behavior where it helps, expose meaningful model choices, and retain improved alternatives even when uncapped FPS alone does not reveal their benefit.

The live integration boundary remains separate: original KSP force queues/providers may feed one aggregate, but original mass writers, constituent velocity/COM readers, orbit tracking, frame corrections, topology and exit restoration must be routed coherently. Aliasing every `part.rb` to one body or merely setting old bodies kinematic does not establish that ownership.
